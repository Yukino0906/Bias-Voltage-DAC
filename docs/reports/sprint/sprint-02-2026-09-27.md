## 2 Evidence of Progress

Assessment weight: 40%

Environment and ideal baseline: I established the ASICFab Virtuoso/Spectre setup, resolved the OA_HOME and model-path issues, and verified an ideal 3-bit charge-redistribution DAC. Eight equal capacitors form C, 2C, 4C, and a C dummy. Code 100 produces approximately 0.5 V with 1 V and 0 V references.

PDK capacitor implementation: I created cap_unit_n65 and cdac_3b_n65 while retaining the ideal switches. Each unit uses mimcap_3t_9m_1p0, with 10 um x 10 um geometry, nominal capacitance of 110.659 fF, and its substrate terminal grounded. At tt_lib and 27 C, Vout is 499.996 mV at 79.581 ns, approximately 4 uV below the ideal code-100 value. Netlist inspection confirms the mimcap_sin_3t model and mismatch disabled; this single-code result does not establish INL/DNL.

Transmission gate tests: I built tg_n65 using core nch/pch devices with bulks tied to VSS/VDD. With 1 V supplies and a single PDK capacitor load, increasing NMOS/PMOS widths from 0.2/0.2 um to 1/2 um, at L = 60 nm, visibly improved tracking of the 1 ns input edges. This comparison changes both absolute size and width ratio; it does not isolate the benefit of a 2:1 ratio.

Hold behavior: Complementary controls turn the TG off at 20-21 ns. Vhold rises from about 1.0000 V to 1.0032 V, a net turn-off step of about +3.2 mV consistent with combined charge injection and clock feedthrough. When the input falls at 31-32 ns, Vhold remains near 1.0032 V; additional coupling is not resolved at the displayed scale. The 45 ns test does not establish long-term retention.
