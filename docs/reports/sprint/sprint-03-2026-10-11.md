Switching disturbance and sampled linearity

October 11, 011 to 100: break at 190 ns, make at 195 ns. Near-minimum marker: 243.21157 mV at 196.06593 ns, approximately 131.79 mV below ideal code 011. This is a sampled local minimum, not a converged peak search.

At 196.0659 ns, bottom plates b0/b1/b2 are 173.7483/436.5179/224.7282 mV. Their weighted sum (b0 + 2b1 + 4b2)/8 = 243.2121125 mV agrees with Vout = 243.2116 mV to about 0.51 uV at displayed precision. The whole-window residual is visually about −4.9 to −0.5 uV. Unequal bottom-plate trajectories account for the dip; their physical cause is not yet isolated.

Error is relative to k × 125 mV. Endpoint step L = (V7−V0)/7 = 124.999568714 mV; INL(k) = (Vk−V0−kL)/L; DNL(k) = (Vk−V(k−1))/L−1. Both use the same endpoint normalization.

Maximum |INL| = 6.32e−5 LSB (011); maximum |DNL| = 8.85e−5 LSB (010 to 011). All sampled steps are positive. These are finite-delay nominal results; hold-window stability, timestep/tolerance convergence, mismatch, PEX and cryogenic operation remain unverified.

Specifications and area outlook

Specs v0 revision 2 was updated against the mentor guide and three full-text papers. Targets remain provisional: 12-bit resolution over 1 V, 5 uW/channel at 1 kupdate/s including allocated support power, <=10 us room-temperature settling to a 61 uV band, and <=61 uV hold drift over 1 ms. These are design goals, not measured achievements.

Area assumes binary weighting plus one dummy and unchanged 10 um × 10 um, 110.659 fF MIM units. It excludes layout enclosure/spacing, routing, edge dummies, switches and support blocks. It is not post-layout core area. The inspected unit has With Mismatch Effect unchecked; this alone does not audit every instance or Monte Carlo setup.

3 Blockers

Design decisions: confirm switch device/drive options, actual load and voltage range, allowable area and validated cryogenic models. High midrange Ron is a concern to evaluate against settling, not proof of specification failure. The bottom-plate response explains the output dip, but the origin of unequal trajectories and a verified remedy remain open.

Confirmed October 11 conditions: tt_lib, 27 C, 1 V supply/reference span, and no added CDAC output load. The array uses eight 110.659 fF units and six MOS TG reference switches; reset and references remain ideal, with Verilog-A control.

Numerical evidence still needed: exported current waveforms, maxstep/tolerance settings, and convergence checks. No power, 1 ms retention, Monte Carlo, PEX or cryogenic qualification is claimed.

4 Proposed Goals for the Next Sprint

Agree with the mentor on TG device/drive direction and the area/load budget; retain the original regression.

Verify disturbance and sampled linearity convergence; complete isolated-bit/reverse tests and one controlled drive-strength comparison.

Implement and compare a physical reset; select one approved functional submodule. The two-channel hold bank remains a concept and requires a suitable DAC drive interface.

Measure loaded settling and hold drift against the working specifications, and check a real MIM layout footprint before choosing higher-resolution architecture.

Application reference: van Staveren et al., Cryo-CMOS Bias-Voltage Generation and Demultiplexing at mK Temperatures for Large-Scale Arrays of Quantum Devices, IEEE TQE, 2025, DOI 10.1109/TQE.2025.3580377.
